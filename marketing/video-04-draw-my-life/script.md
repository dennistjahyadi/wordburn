# Video 04 — Draw my life

A voiceover for a hand-drawn "draw my life" animation. Dennis narrates in the
first person: the person who needed captions and ended up building the app.
Written 2026-10-03 against app 1.0.7: three free videos with a watermark, Pro as
a subscription, all languages free, batch built but not yet run on a phone.

Every claim follows `reports/Caption app apple to apple comparison.md`, which
lists what an ad may say. It has no speed or accuracy claims, no counts set
against rivals, never says "only" or "cheapest", always puts the watermark next
to "free", and names no competitor.

**Lines 1 and 2 are yours to correct.** They tell a story about you, and they
have to be the true one. Keep the order (the need, the pain, building it) and
change the details.

## Lines that are ready but held back

- **Batch:** "Got twenty clips? Queue them all, pick one look, and put the phone
  down." Put it after line 10 of A once batch has run on the A54. It has only run
  on an emulator, and the report holds it out of ads until then.
- **Languages:** "Speak Spanish, German or Indonesian? One download and it
  captions those too." This one is true now. It is left out because any count
  invites a comparison with rivals' 100+, and a list of three names avoids that
  only partly.

## Lines not to add

- "Pay once, keep it forever" or "no subscription". Pro is a subscription now.
  **The four video-03 ads (`payonce`, `nointernet`, `nocredits`, `justcaptions`)
  all say this and should not be posted as they are.**
- "Free" with no mention of the watermark, and "unlimited" for the free tier. A
  free user gets three videos.
- "Fast", "accurate", "the only", "pixel-perfect". Say "matches" instead.
