# Video 04 — image prompts, one per scene

One prompt for each scene of [script.md](script.md), version A (60 s). Each
prompt is complete on its own and can be pasted straight into the generator:
the style and the character are written into every one, because most
generators do not carry anything from one image to the next.

## Before you start

- **Generate the character first.** Use the character prompt below, keep the
  best result, and feed it back as a reference for every scene (Midjourney
  `--cref`, ChatGPT/Gemini "use this character", Leonardo character
  reference). Without a reference the character changes from scene to scene.
- **Describe yourself in the character line.** It says "a young person" with
  short messy hair. Change it to match you (glasses, beard, hair, hoodie
  colour), since the story is yours.
- **Leave the words out of the picture and add them in the edit.** Image models
  misspell text. The prompts ask for blank word pills, and the real words
  ("Wordbern" → "Wordburn" in scene 8) go on afterwards as text layers.
- **Do not let the generator draw Google Play's logo or any rival's icon.** In
  scene 13, put Google's official "Get it on Google Play" badge on top of the
  blank space. The rival apps in scene 2 are blank icons on purpose.
- **Composition is 9:16 with the top and bottom fifth left empty**, for
  TikTok's buttons and the subtitles. Change the aspect ratio in the style line
  if you cut it 16:9 instead.
- **Colour has one job.** Black marker for everything, coral red for the pain
  (credits, waiting), and bright yellow only for Wordburn. The colour shift
  from the first half to the second tells the story without any words.

```
hand-drawn "draw my life" whiteboard doodle, thick black marker lines on plain white paper, simple flat shapes, slightly wobbly hand-drawn lines, no shading, lots of white space, the only accent colours are bright yellow (#FFE03D) and coral red (#FF7A66), no gradients, no photorealism, no 3D, no text, vertical 9:16, subject in the middle three-fifths, top and bottom fifth left empty white
```

generator draws it badly, leave the space empty and put `store/wordburn-mark.svg`
over it in the edit.

## Real assets: logo and app screens

Draw the story and show the product. Scenes 1–4, 6 and 9 stay fully drawn.
In the scenes that make a claim, real app screens go inside a hand-drawn phone
frame (or the drawn phone slides away to reveal them). A doodle of the app
proves nothing, and a doodle that differs from the app is a misleading-ad
problem on Play.

For scenes 7, 8, 10 and 11, add this to the end of the prompt so there is a
clean space for the real screen: `the phone screen is drawn as an empty
rectangle, completely blank white inside, facing the viewer straight on`.

## Making it look drawn

The prompts produce each scene as a finished drawing. To make it look like it is
being drawn, use a whiteboard tool (VideoScribe, Doodly) or reverse an erase:
record the frame being wiped stroke by stroke and play it backwards. Either way,
a finished still is the right thing to generate.
