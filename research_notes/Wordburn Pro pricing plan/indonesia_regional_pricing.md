# Regional and purchasing-power pricing for Google Play subscriptions — Indonesia first, other emerging markets second

Research date: 2026-10-02. Every IDR figure carries the date it was observed in the cited source. Wordburn's current state, for reference: USD 4.99 / 9.99 / 39.99 placeholders auto-converted by Play to **Rp 90.000 / Rp 179.000 / Rp 690.000** on an A54 in Indonesia (observed 2026-10-01, from CLAUDE.md).

The creator-tool price band in Indonesia on the stores runs from roughly **Rp 41.000 to Rp 129.000 a month**, with the category leader (CapCut Pro) at the top of it at Rp 127–129k/month and ~Rp 1.1M/year, and the big consumer-subscription anchors (Spotify, YouTube Premium, Netflix Mobile) at **Rp 60–70k a month**. ## 2. How Play's auto-conversion works vs. hand-set local prices; IDR minimums and rounding; purchasing-power recommendations

### Takeaway
Play converts the default price at the exchange rate on the day you set it, adds tax in tax-inclusive countries (Indonesia is one), rounds to local price patterns, then **freezes** the result until you click "Update exchange rates"; nothing in the official help describes a purchasing-power-based recommendation. The claim that Play Console "suggests" PPP prices comes only from third-party pricing-tool blogs. Any Indonesian price below exchange parity has to be typed in by hand, per country, per base plan.

### Cited Findings
- Play uses the default price as the base and generates local prices by "converting your price to the local currency, adding tax in select countries, and applying locally relevant pricing patterns" using "valid exchange rates for the date on which you set the price". Local prices can be overridden by hand (pencil icon in the Price column), and "Update exchange rates" refreshes the converted set to current rates and "country-specific pricing patterns" without touching the base price — [Play Console Help: Set up your app's prices](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6334373?hl=en)
- That page contains **no mention** of price suggestions based on purchasing power; it links out to "a list of currencies and price ranges allowed by country" — [same page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6334373?hl=en)
- Converted prices do not track the rate afterwards: "it does not keep re-adjusting as exchange rates move; it refreshes only when you ask." The number generated for India or Indonesia "is roughly equivalent to your USD price at current exchange rates, with local tax adjustments folded in" — [PricePush, 6 May 2026](https://pricepush.app/blog/google-play-iap-pricing-by-country) (secondary; a pricing-tool vendor)
- Third-party vendors claim Play shows PPP-based suggestions — e.g. "$2.99 ... might mathematically convert to ₹262 in India, but Google suggests ₹199" and "$19.99 ... auto-converts to roughly ₹1,900 in India while the PPP-appropriate price is closer to ₹650 to ₹700" — [regionalpricecalculator.com](https://regionalpricecalculator.com/blog/google-play-pricing-strategy.html); [Mirava](https://www.mirava.io/blog/how-does-localized-pricing-work-in-google-play). **Not corroborated by any Google page found**; treat as vendor marketing until seen in Console.
- Google removed "pricing templates" from Play Console, so bulk-applying one localised price set across products is no longer a built-in feature — [PricePush newsletter](https://newsletter.pricepush.app/p/google-play-pricing-templates-are)
- Play Console offers **price experiments** for in-app products (A/B price tests per country) — [Android Developers Blog, May 2023](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2023/05/price-experiments-in-play-console.html)
- Historic minimums: in Nov 2015 Google cut minimums in 17 countries, Indonesia's to **Rp 3.000**, Brazil R$0.99, Mexico MX$5, Philippines ₱15, Turkey ₺0.59, Vietnam ₫6.000 — [Android Developers Blog, Nov 2015](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2015/11/minimum-purchase-price-for-apps-and-in.html); [Sensor Tower summary](https://sensortower.com/blog/google-play-store-app-pricing). In March 2021 Google extended "10–30 cents US equivalent" minimums to 20 more markets and listed Indonesia, India, Brazil, Mexico, Philippines, Turkey among markets that already had sub-dollar pricing — [Android Developers Blog, 4 Mar 2021](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2021/03/sub-dollar-pricing-expansion-in-20.html)
- Google's own emerging-market tiering (as described by a third party): "Emerging Markets (India, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Egypt, Nigeria, Pakistan at 20–40% of Tier 1 pricing)" and "Mid-Income (Mexico, Turkey, Poland, Malaysia, Thailand at 40–60%)" — [regionalpricecalculator.com](https://regionalpricecalculator.com/blog/google-play-pricing-strategy.html) (secondary, unverified)
- Google at I/O 2025 "expanded pricing arbitrage protection to more countries, automatically detecting and blocking abusive purchases that exploit regional price differences", added **payment-location restrictions** in Play Console for limited-availability content, and strengthened free-trial/intro-price abuse protection — [Google Play, Q2 2025 update](https://google.play/the-latest/2025-q2/); [Android Authority, 23 May 2025](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-play-regional-pricing-abuse-protection-3560805/)

### Inferences
- The observed conversions imply **Rp 18.036 / 17.918 / 17.254 per USD** for the weekly / monthly / yearly (90.000/4.99, 179.000/9.99, 690.000/39.99). Dividing by 1.11 for the VAT gross-up gives Rp 16.249 / 16.142 / 15.544 — consistent with Play taking a market rate in the low-to-mid 16.000s, adding 11% PPN, and rounding to a "pretty" local price point (90k, 179k, 690k). The yearly rounded down more than the others, i.e. Play's pattern rounding is coarse at six figures.
- Because converted prices freeze on the day they were set, and the rupiah has moved, the three numbers on the phone are whatever rate applied when the placeholders were entered; they will not change until someone presses "Update exchange rates" or types a local price.
- Setting IDR by hand is the only route to a purchasing-power price. There is no risk of falling below the IDR minimum at any plausible Wordburn price (the floor is on the order of Rp 3.000).

### Gaps
- The **current official IDR min/max and rounding rules** could not be read: the table on "Supported locations for distribution to Google Play users" (help answer 138294 / table 3541286, and play.google.com/supported-locations) is rendered client-side and every fetch returned only the page chrome. Check it in Console, or in a browser.
- Whether Play Console today shows any "recommended" local price beside the auto-converted one: no primary source. Look at the per-country price editor for a base plan in Console.
- Exact rounding conventions Play applies to IDR (to the nearest Rp 1.000? Rp 500? ending in 9?) — undocumented; the three observed prices end in 0.000, 9.000 and 0.000.

## 3. Indonesia's purchasing-power factor and what PPP tools imply for price as a fraction of US price

### Takeaway
On the World Bank's 2025 private-consumption PPP factor, one international dollar buys in Indonesia what **Rp 5.066** buys — against a market rate in the Rp 16.000s, a price level of roughly **0.31** of the US. The Big Mac index puts the rupiah 57% undervalued (ratio ~0.43). Subscription-app medians in India/SEA actually sit at **~37–54%** of North American prices, so the empirical band for "what the market bears" is 0.35–0.50 of US, not the 0.31 of strict PPP.

### Cited Findings
- World Bank, PPP conversion factor for private consumption (LCU per international $), Indonesia: **2025: 5.065,97**; 2024: 5.104,98; 2023: 5.143,35; 2022: 5.165,53 — [World Bank API, PA.NUS.PRVT.PP, ID](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/ID/indicator/PA.NUS.PRVT.PP?format=json&mrv=4)
- Same indicator, 2025, for the secondary markets: **Brazil 2,58 BRL**, **India 19,84 INR**, **Philippines 20,53 PHP**, **Mexico 11,31 MXN**, **Türkiye 17,74 TRY**, **Viet Nam 7.203,37 VND** per international $ — [World Bank API, ID;BR;IN;PH](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/ID;BR;IN;PH/indicator/PA.NUS.PRVT.PP?format=json&mrv=1&per_page=10); [World Bank API, VN;MX;TR](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/VN;MX;TR/indicator/PA.NUS.PRVT.PP?format=json&mrv=1&per_page=10)
- GDP-based PPP factor for Indonesia, 2024: 4.747,91 IDR per international $ — [World Bank, PA.NUS.PPP](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/PA.NUS.PPP?locations=ID) (via search summary)
- GDP per capita, current US$, 2025: **Indonesia $5.060**, **United States $90.027** — about 18× — [World Bank API, NY.GDP.PCAP.CD](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/ID;US/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD?format=json&mrv=1&per_page=20)
- Big Mac index, July 2025: rupiah **57.0% undervalued**, second only to the Egyptian pound; a Big Mac cost $2,54 in Indonesia earlier in 2025 — [The Burger Index 2024–25 rankings](https://www.theburgerindex.com/blog/currency-rankings-2025); [Statbase Big Mac data](https://statbase.org/datasets/indexes-and-ratings/bigmac-index/)
- ParityDeals' PPP calculator: Rp 333.109.067 (~$19.947) in Yogyakarta buys what $70.000 buys in New York — a ratio of **0.285** — [ParityDeals US vs Indonesia](https://www.paritydeals.com/ppp-calculator/united-states-vs-indonesia/?salary=70000&source_city=new-york&target_city=yogyakarta). ParityDeals groups countries into PPP bands and its example rule is a "40% discount to Developing regions (PPP 0.3–0.4)" — [ParityDeals docs](https://docs.paritydeals.com/dynamic-pricing/purchaisng-parity-power-pricing)
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026, median prices, **India & SE Asia vs North America**: weekly **$4.61 vs $7.03**, monthly **$3.75 vs $9.99**, yearly **$18.32 vs $39.99** — i.e. 66% / 38% / 46% of NA — [RevenueCat SoSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)

### Inferences
- Strict PPP translation of Wordburn's USD prices (at the 2025 private-consumption factor of Rp 5.066 per $): **$4.99 → Rp 25.300; $9.99 → Rp 50.600; $39.99 → Rp 202.600**. That is 28–29% of what Play auto-converted.
- Big Mac translation (0.43): Rp 39k / 77k / 297k. RevenueCat-median translation (what apps in the region actually charge): **~Rp 75k weekly / ~Rp 61k monthly / ~Rp 300k yearly** at a rough Rp 16.300/$ (rate not sourced; see gap).
- The comparables in section 1 cluster exactly where the RevenueCat medians land: Rp 41–99k a month, Rp 139–800k a year. Strict PPP would put Wordburn *below* InShot, which is not where a Pro tier of a one-person app wants to be; the Big Mac/RevenueCat band is the realistic target.
- A reasonable Indonesian tier, pending Dennis's call: monthly in the **Rp 49.000–69.000** range (Zeemo-entry to YouTube Premium), yearly at roughly 5–6 months' worth (**Rp 299.000–399.000**, beside Zeemo's Rp 399k), weekly only if kept — at **Rp 19.000–29.000**, under Canva's Rp 39k. Note RevenueCat's finding that the yearly in IN/SEA is 4.9× the monthly, vs 4.0× in NA: the region buys yearly less, so the annual discount can be shallower.

### Gaps
- No sourced **spot USD/IDR rate** for 2026-10-02 was captured; all IDR-from-USD arithmetic above uses either the PPP factor directly or the rate implied by Play's own conversion. Replace with the day's rate before quoting.
- IMF's own implied PPP rate for 2025 was not fetched separately (World Bank is the primary used).
- No Paddle or Stripe published dataset with an Indonesia-specific localisation multiplier was found.

## 4. Indonesian willingness to pay: ARPU, payment methods, conversion vs. US

### Takeaway
Every regional benchmark says the same thing: Indonesia/SEA converts at a quarter to a half of North America and pays about half per subscription, so a paying user is worth roughly **$14 in year one vs $32** in NA. Payment rails are not the bottleneck — Play Indonesia takes GoPay, DANA, OVO, ShopeePay, DOKU and carrier billing on all five operators — but there is no public figure for what share of Play transactions each rail carries.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat SoSA 2026, IN/SEA vs NA: download-to-trial (D30) **3.0–3.7% vs 7.1%**; trial-to-paid **15.2% vs 34.2%**; download-to-paid (D35) **0.7% vs 2.8%**; revenue per install D14 **$0.08 vs $0.38**, D60 **$0.11 vs $0.55**; realised LTV per payer after one year **$14 vs $32**; weekly-plan share of subscriptions sold 16–29% in IN/SEA; yearly share 19–40% — [RevenueCat SoSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- RevenueCat SoSA 2025: D35 download-to-paid 2.6% NA vs **1.4% IN/SEA**; LTV per payer after 1 year lowest in IN/SEA at **$17.46 (iOS) and $11.52 (Google Play)**; IN/SEA **refund rate 7.7%** vs 3.4% NA; weekly plans 36% of the IN/SEA mix — [RevenueCat SoSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- Play Indonesia accepted payment methods: e-wallets **DANA, DOKU, GoPay, OVO, ShopeePay**; carrier billing on **Indosat, Smartfren, Telkomsel, XL/AXIS, Tri** (app only, not web); cards JCB, Mastercard, Visa — [Google Play Help, accepted payment methods, Indonesia](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2651410?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DID)
- Google, on carrier billing: "In Indonesia, the number of people who make app and in-app purchases quadrupled since direct carrier billing was launched there in March 2015" — [Google blog](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-asia/pay-for-google-play-content-in-india/)
- Telkomsel launched Play DCB in Feb 2015 for its ~20M Android users (55% of its smartphone base); Indosat was first in 2014 — [Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/telkomsel-carrier-billing-google-play/); [Investor.id](https://investor.id/telecommunication/108808/telkomsel-luncurkan-google-play-store-direct-carries-billing)
- E-wallet reach, 2024 survey: 88% of respondents use GoPay, ~83% DANA; for online transactions ShopeePay is most used (68%), then GoPay 17%, DANA 10%, OVO 2%; ~144M digital-payment users in Indonesia in 2024 — [Antom, Indonesia payment methods](https://knowledge.antom.com/top-digital-payment-methods-in-indonesia-that-power-e-commerce); [Ipsos 2026 e-wallet survey](https://www.ipsos.com/en-id/mapping-digital-wallet-landscape-2026-which-platform-leads-users-preferred-choice-according-ipsos)
- Sensor Tower: Indonesia accounted for **5.7B downloads, 7.0% of Google Play downloads in 2025** (−0.3% YoY), and ranked second globally after India in time spent in apps — [GameDev Reports on Sensor Tower State of Mobile 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-the-state-of-the-mobile); [Sensor Tower 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-state-of-mobile-consumers-usd150-billion-spent-on-mobile-highlights)

### Inferences
- The weekly plan is disproportionately important in this region (16–36% of the mix across the two reports, vs the yearly's 19–40%), so getting the **weekly** price right in Indonesia matters more than it would in the US. At Rp 90.000 it is currently the plan most out of line with the market.
- A trial-to-paid of 15% and a refund rate of 7.7% mean a yearly-with-trial in Indonesia converts a fifth as well as in NA per trial start; a weekly without a trial may well out-earn it.
- With rails this broad, "no card" is not why an Indonesian user declines; price is.

### Gaps
- No public figure for the **share of Play transactions in Indonesia by rail** (carrier billing vs GoPay/DANA vs card). Google does not publish it; the operator press releases are from 2015.
- No Indonesia-only (as opposed to IN/SEA pooled) conversion or ARPU benchmark was found. RevenueCat pools India with SE Asia; India's ARPU drags the pool down, so Indonesia alone is probably somewhat better than these medians, but no source says by how much.
- No Sensor Tower / data.ai figure for Indonesian **consumer spend** (IAP revenue) in 2024–25 was found in accessible form; only downloads and time spent.

## 5. Indonesia's short-form creator and "clipper" market

### Takeaway
Indonesia is TikTok's second-largest market by users (100M+ active, ~45 hours a month each in late 2024), with creators earning mostly from brand deals and TikTok Shop rather than view payouts, which run at some of the lowest CPMs in the world. Nothing found quantifies what Indonesian creators pay for editing tools beyond the store prices in section 1 and the very active grey market for CapCut/Canva accounts.

### Cited Findings
- "Indonesia remains TikTok's second largest market. More than 100 million Indonesian users spent an average of roughly 9 percent of their waking lives just using TikTok in November 2024"; average user ~45 hours in the app that month; reported ad reach in Indonesia dropped >30% in early 2025, likely from removal of duplicate/inauthentic accounts — [DataReportal, Digital 2025 top social platforms](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-sub-section-top-social-platforms)
- Start of 2025: TikTok ads reached 53.5% of Indonesian adults 18+ — [DataReportal Digital 2025 global overview](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-global-overview-report)
- Other counts in circulation: ~108M active users; "fourth-largest market, 125M+" — [Teleprompter TikTok statistics 2025](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-statistics); [The Global Statistics, Indonesia TikTok 2026](https://www.theglobalstatistics.com/indonesia-tiktok-user-statistics/) (both secondary aggregators; they disagree with each other and with DataReportal on rank)
- CPM: "India and Indonesia have the lowest CPMs, averaging between $0.005–$0.01" — [Awisee, TikTok CPM 2025](https://awisee.com/blog/tiktok-cpm/) (secondary)
- Indonesian creator income: brand deals "Rp500,000 to Rp30,000,000 per video" and TikTok Shop as the dominant revenue stream — [ttcalculator.net, TikTok pay Indonesia](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/tiktok-pay-indonesia-earnings/) (weak source, a calculator site; no methodology)
- Clipper economics (global, not Indonesia): "median full-time clipper makes $300 to $2,500 per month on solo CPM deals" — [forkoff.xyz, clipper earnings 2026](https://forkoff.xyz/blog/clipping/how-much-do-clippers-earn-2026) (weak source)
- Grey market for creator-tool subscriptions is large and visible: reseller sites sell CapCut Pro and Canva Pro "murah, legal, bergaransi" — [freemium.id](https://freemium.id/blog/murah-banget-langganan-capcut-pro-di-sini); [temanakun.com CapCut Pro](https://temanakun.com/product/capcut-pro/); [Canva Pro Rp 20rb/bln](https://www.literally-apps.id/blog/harga-canva-pro-naik-2026-indonesia); and "cara bayar Spotify/YouTube Premium murah" guides are a genre — [Tuwaga](https://tuwaga.id/artikel/cara-bayar-spotify-youtube-premium-murah/)
- Local caption competitor on Play: "Auto Captions: Caption Video", pitched at Indonesian social-media creators, 99+ languages — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.autocaptions.app&hl=id) (price not retrievable)

### Inferences
- The grey market (shared/family-plan reselling at Rp 20–50k/month for tools whose list price is Rp 95–129k) is the clearest signal of the Indonesian clearing price for a creator tool: **Rp 20.000–50.000 a month** is what a large population will actually pay, and the official Rp 95–129k is tolerated by a minority.
- With CPMs at $0.005–0.01, an Indonesian clipper's view-based income cannot justify a $10/month tool; a Rp 179k monthly is more than many creators' monthly TikTok view payout. The buyers who can justify Pro are brand-deal and TikTok Shop sellers, which is the audience the listing should speak to.

### Gaps
- No survey or press piece quantifying **what Indonesian creators spend on editing tools** was found (DailySocial / Katadata / Tech in Asia searches did not surface one).
- No Indonesia-specific clipper-economy figures; the clipper data found is global and from low-authority sources.
- No sizing of the local competitor apps' revenue.

## 6. Lessons from developers who set regional prices: revenue effects and risks

### Takeaway
The public evidence says that halving prices in low-income tiers roughly doubles conversion (SuperScale: 0.79% → 1.37%) and that Spotify's $1.45 India plan grew revenue 92.6% over four years — but these are vendor-curated anecdotes, and no apples-to-apples indie revenue case for Indonesia was found. The main risk, VPN arbitrage, is now mitigated on Play's side: since May 2025 Google blocks purchases that exploit regional price gaps and lets developers restrict payment location.

### Cited Findings
- Spotify India at "$1.45/month vs $9.99 in the US" yielded "92.6% revenue growth and 26% market share within 4 years" (attributed to Billboard Pro); SuperScale: "50% price reduction in Tier 3 countries increased conversion rates from 0.79% to 1.37%" (+73%); Duolingo rest-of-world revenue +45% in 2022 — [Mirava, regional pricing boosts revenue](https://www.mirava.io/blog/how-regional-pricing-boosts-revenue-for-mobile-apps) (secondary; vendor blog quoting others)
- Claims of "35–50% total revenue uplift" from regional pricing, Duolingo "+400% paid subs from emerging markets" at ₹549, Flo "+80% in non-English markets vs 35%" — [regionalpricecalculator.com](https://regionalpricecalculator.com/blog/google-play-pricing-strategy.html); [Mirava FAQ](https://www.mirava.io/blog/app-pricing-strategy-common-questions-answered). **Unverified**: Duolingo's own shareholder letters searched do not state the ₹549 figure or the 400% claim — [Duolingo IR](https://investors.duolingo.com/)
- Google I/O 2025: "expanded pricing arbitrage protection to more countries, automatically detecting and blocking abusive purchases that exploit regional price differences. This helps developers confidently offer localized pricing"; plus **payment location restrictions in Play Console** and stronger free-trial abuse protection — [Google Play Q2 2025](https://google.play/the-latest/2025-q2/); [Android Authority, 23 May 2025](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-play-regional-pricing-abuse-protection-3560805/)
- Before that, users openly used VPNs to buy subscriptions priced for Nigeria or India, and "cheapest country for IAP" guides rank Indonesia among the cheap storefronts — [VPNCentral](https://vpncentral.com/cheapest-country-in-app-purchases/); [HackerNoon arbitrage detection](https://hackernoon.com/detecting-arbitrage-opportunities-in-global-digital-product-markets)
- RevenueCat's regional medians (section 3) show that developers in aggregate *do* price IN/SEA at 38–66% of NA, i.e. regional pricing is the norm, not the exception — [RevenueCat SoSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Play price experiments let a developer test two prices per country on in-app products — [Android Developers Blog, May 2023](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2023/05/price-experiments-in-play-console.html)

### Inferences
- For Wordburn specifically, arbitrage risk is small: the app is Android-only, its US price is $9.99/month, and a Play account's country can only be changed once a year and requires a local payment method — on top of Google's 2025 detection. An Indonesian price at 35–50% of US is well inside the gap every large app already runs.
- The Play rule that matters is not a prohibition on regional pricing (it is encouraged) but that prices must be tax-inclusive where Google collects tax, and that a subscription price change for existing subscribers triggers Play's price-change consent flow — set the IDR price *before* the first Indonesian subscriber rather than after.

### Gaps
- No **indie, Android, Indonesia** case study with before/after revenue was found; every public case is a large app or a vendor composite. Mirava's and regionalpricecalculator's numbers cannot be traced to primary reports.
- Google's own statement of which countries the arbitrage protection now covers was not found; the I/O coverage says "more countries" without a list.

## 7. Google Play's 2025–2026 fee structure for subscriptions, and Indonesian PPN

### Takeaway
Outside the US/UK/EEA/Australia/Japan — so in Indonesia — the Play fee on auto-renewing subscriptions is **15%** from the first dollar. Indonesia's price is **tax-inclusive** and Google collects and remits **11% PPN** inside it. Of a tax-inclusive Rp 179.000, roughly **Rp 137.000 (76.6%)** reaches the developer's Play payout before any withholding or FX.

### Cited Findings
- Service fees: subscriptions in "specified regions (Australia, EEA, Japan, UK, US)" pay "10% + 5% billing fee" for the first $1M; "for all other markets: 15% regardless of revenue threshold"; the 15% tier applies to "the first $1M (USD) revenue earned by the developer each year" and 30% above it (non-subscription); rollout "June 30, 2026 for the EEA, UK, and US; September 30, 2026 for Australia and Japan" — [Play Console Help, Service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en)
- "This 10% service fee also applies to all auto-renewing subscriptions"; "In the United States, United Kingdom, and the European Economic Area, the billing fee is set at 5%"; "We'll announce billing fee details for other markets soon" — [Android Developers Blog, 24 Jun 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/06/play-expanded-billing.html); [Android Developers Blog, Mar 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/03/a-new-era-for-choice-and-openness.html)
- "99% of developers who are subject to a service fee qualify for a fee of 15% or less" — [Play Console Help, understanding service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11131145?hl=en)
- Indonesia tax: "Google is responsible for determining, charging, and remitting **11% VAT**" on paid apps and IAP via Play billing, so the developer need not remit it; pricing is tax-inclusive. Also: Mexico 16% VAT (Google remits), Vietnam 5–10% depending on developer status, India GST with Google deducting withholding and GST TCS; Brazil, Philippines, Türkiye listed as Google-remitted without a rate on the page — [Play Console Help, tax rates and VAT](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/138000?hl=en)
- Indonesian VAT on Play began at 10% on 1 Aug 2020; "VAT may be calculated and charged using existing prices for subscription products", and developers may "publish ... new subscription products with a different price point or change existing prices" — [Google Play developer notice, 2020](https://groups.google.com/a/opass.app/g/dev/c/L1QHEzyczOE)
- Play briefly charged **12%** PPN from 2 Jan 2025 and reverted to **11%** by 7 Jan 2025 after the Finance Ministry said 12% applies only to luxury goods — [Kompas Tekno, 2 Jan 2025](https://tekno.kompas.com/read/2025/01/02/11023837/google-play-store-indonesia-tetap-kenakan-ppn-12-persen?page=all); [Kompas Tekno, 7 Jan 2025](https://tekno.kompas.com/read/2025/01/07/13080057/ppn-google-play-store-indonesia-kembali-normal-jadi-11-persen)
- Third-party reading of the 2026 change: the headline 10% plus the 5% billing fee "keeps the effective rate at 15% (unchanged since 2022)" — [PricePush, Play fees 2026](https://pricepush.app/blog/google-play-subscription-fees-2026-real-math); [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/google-play-billing-changes-subscriptions-fees/)

### Inferences
- Net-per-subscription arithmetic for Indonesia, assuming the 15% is taken on the ex-VAT amount (Google's pages do not say; see gap):
  - Rp 90.000 weekly → ex-PPN Rp 81.081 → −15% → **Rp 68.919** to the developer.
  - Rp 179.000 monthly → Rp 161.261 → **Rp 137.072**.
  - Rp 690.000 yearly → Rp 621.622 → **Rp 528.378**.
  - Generic: **developer net ≈ 0.766 × sticker** in Indonesia, vs 0.85 × sticker in a no-VAT US state.
- A hand-set Rp 59.000 monthly would net ≈ Rp 45.200; Rp 349.000 yearly ≈ Rp 267.300. At those prices one Indonesian subscriber-month is worth about a third of a US one, which is exactly the LTV gap RevenueCat reports (section 4), so the lower price does not make the Indonesian user worse than the benchmark — it makes the Indonesian user *exist*.
- The June 2026 fee change does **not** apply to Indonesia yet; the "all other markets: 15%" line is what governs Wordburn's Indonesian subscriptions today, and the billing-fee split for other markets is unannounced.

### Gaps
- Google's pages do not state whether the 15% service fee is computed on the gross or the ex-VAT amount; the payout reports in Console will show it (the tax line is itemised there). The 0.766 figure assumes ex-VAT, which is the usual treatment.
- Any Indonesian **withholding tax** or income-tax treatment on Play payouts to an Indonesian-resident developer (PPh) was out of scope and not researched.
- Whether Play will extend the 10% + billing-fee structure to Indonesia, and at what billing fee, is unannounced as of the June 2026 post.
