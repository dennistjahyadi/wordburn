# What users of caption-first apps complain about and praise (2025–2026)

Scope: Captions (captions.ai / Mirage, incl. Captions Lite), Zeemo, AutoCap, SubCap, MixCaptions, Submagic, Kaptioned, Zubtitle, "Offline AI Auto Captions" (Bytesweavers). CapCut/VN and Opus/Klap excluded except as one-line asides. Researched 2026-10-02. Method note: Trustpilot, App Store (via Apple's review page and justuseapp mirrors), Capterra and Google Play listings were fetched directly where the fetch tool could read them; Google Play's own pages returned truncated and could not be quoted verbatim, so Play-side evidence is thinner and is often a search-engine summary of the listing rather than a direct quote. Reddit was effectively unreachable through the search tool (every `site:reddit.com` query returned App Store listings instead), so creator-forum evidence is a declared gap, not a finding of absence.

## Q1. Most frequent complaints per app in 1–2 star reviews

### Takeaway
Across every cloud caption app the dominant 1-star complaint is money, not captions: credits or minutes consumed by failed or useless runs, charges continuing after cancellation, renewals without notice, and trials that end in a bill. Processing-never-starts / export-blocked is the second cluster, and it is usually reported *because* it cost credits. Pure accuracy complaints exist but are a minority and are often softened ("I can edit it").

### Inferences
- Frequency ranking (by how often a theme appears across the apps above): (1) credits/minutes consumed, including by failed runs; (2) continued billing after cancel / renewal without notice / trial converting to a charge; (3) processing never starts, export blocked, or app crashes mid-transcription; (4) ads and watermark in free tiers (Android-heavy: Zeemo, AutoCap, Kaptioned); (5) unreachable or slow support; (6) accuracy; (7) feature gaps (words-per-line control, fonts, RTL, cut/paste).
- Severity ranking (by the language used and the money at stake): billing-after-cancel and surprise renewals (hundreds of euros, "theifs", "scam") > credits lost to failures > product outages > watermark/ads > accuracy.
- Accuracy is a smaller share of 1-star reviews than a caption developer would expect; the same reviewers who note misses often add that they can edit the result. The exception is AutoCap, where "correct every other word" appears alongside a no-audio-during-edit complaint.
- "Export not matching preview" and "timing drift" did not surface as named complaints in any source read. Either they are rare, or users describe them as "export error"/"doesn't work" without the diagnosis.

### Gaps
- Google Play review text for Captions Lite, Zeemo (Android), SubCap, AutoCap and Bytesweavers could not be fetched verbatim; the Play findings above are search-engine summaries of those listings and should be treated as paraphrase.
- No per-topic frequency counts (e.g. AppFollow/AppBot percentages) were found publicly for any of these apps.
- Kaptioned's negative-review page (AppGrooves) was unreachable (DNS failure).
- No evidence either way on file-size limits as a complaint theme.

## Q2. What 5-star reviews praise most

### Takeaway
Five-star reviews praise, in order, ease ("so simple", "a breeze"), accuracy ("never gets the words wrong", "catches pretty much everything"), time saved, support responsiveness (Captions and Submagic especially, which suggests support is also what rescues billing complaints), and styles. Speed is rarely named explicitly; "time-saving" stands in for it. Price is praised only at the cheap end (Zeemo "good price too").

### Gaps

## Q3. Complaints about uploading footage, data retention or needing an account

### Takeaway
No explicit privacy complaints ("my footage is on their servers") were found in any review or thread read. The signal is indirect: a dozen App Store listings now sell "videos never leave your device" and "no sign-up" as headline features, which is marketing responding to a concern rather than the concern itself. Account/card gating does draw direct complaints (SubCap).

### Cited Findings
- Listings marketing privacy as a feature: Captionix "videos never leaving your device ... processing everything locally" with "The developer does not collect any data from this app" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6480364240); Caption This "transcribe offline, using Apple's secure speech recognition technology to keep your personal data secure" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/caption-this/id1449087035); one listing: "All video processing happens locally on your device. Your files never leave your phone. There's no sign-up, no cloud storage, and no personal data collected." and another: "Your videos never get sent to the cloud" — [App Store search results](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6752219560); a web tool titled "Free Auto Caption Generator (No Upload, No Signup)" — [vidstudio.app](https://vidstudio.app/auto-caption)
- Bytesweavers' Android listing leads with "your video and audio are not uploaded to a transcription server" — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.bytesweavers.offlineautocaptions)
- Card/account gating complaint: SubCap users say they "must provide a payment card to even try auto-captions" (search summary) — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ratel.subcap&hl=en_US)
- Upload-side friction appears as outage, not privacy: Submagic "website is often down for days at a time" — Jelie J, 8 Jul 2025 — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/submagic.co?stars=1); Captions "generation process never started" — Norbert S, 10 Aug 2026 — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/captions.ai?stars=1); Captions processing "SLOW d⭕️wn ... after your first day" — DARKSHARK, 6 Feb 2025 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/captions-ai-edits-your-video/id1541407007?see-all=reviews)

### Inferences
- Privacy is not a voiced pain in this audience's reviews; it is a sold benefit. In an ad it works as a secondary line ("nothing leaves your phone") rather than as the hook.
- The felt version of "cloud" is reliability: server down, queue stalled, throttled processing. "No upload, no queue, no outage" is the complaint-shaped way to say on-device.
- "No account" has a direct review-shaped enemy in SubCap's card wall and Captions' trial-to-charge stories.

### Gaps
- Reddit, X and TikTok comment threads were not reachable through the search tool; the `site:reddit.com` queries returned App Store listings. Any privacy threads there remain unverified.
- No data-retention complaint or policy dispute was found for any of the nine apps.

## Q4. What creators say about credits and minutes models

### Takeaway
Credits are the single most-resented mechanism in the category: subscribers feel charged twice (a subscription *and* a meter), the meter runs on failures, and Captions' switch to unified credits with the Mirage rebrand produced "5% of what I could do before" reviews. Submagic's per-video quotas and Zeemo's credit-per-minute draw the same complaint in different units.

### Cited Findings
- Captions' current model: "A unified credits system runs across AI features – Transcription, AI Edit, dubbing, and Twin generation" — [Mirage help centre](https://help.mirage.app/docs/ai-usage); Captions rebranded as Mirage on 4 Sep 2025 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/04/captions-rebrands-as-mirage-expands-beyond-creator-tools-to-ai-video-research/); it moved to freemium in Jan 2025 "to better compete with apps like ByteDance's CapCut and Meta's Edits" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/09/video-editing-app-captions-switches-to-a-freemium-model-to-boost-growth)
- "if you exceed your allocated credits for generative AI features, you will incur additional charges" (Max $24.99/mo, Scale $69.99/mo) — [eesel pricing guide](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/captions-ai-pricing)
- "the credit system feels frustrating and misleading, as you pay for a subscription but are still limited by credits that run out quickly, creating a feeling of being charged twice for the same thing" (search summary of App Store reviews) — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/captions-ai-edits-your-video/id1541407007?see-all=reviews)
- Captions credits consumed by AI mistakes: Sacredcellf and C Leam reviews cited in Q1 — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/captions-ai-edits-your-video/id1541407007?see-all=reviews); "the ai glitches then the credits get over" — Piyush Anandani, 25 May 2026 — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/captions.ai?stars=1)
- Zeemo: "300 credit so you can make one video a month" — Yxnes, 19 Sep 2025 — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zeemo.ai); Android reviewers say credits are deducted with no subtitles delivered — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cn.bluepulse.caption&hl=en_CA); "even with a paid monthly subscription, actions are still deducted from a finite pool of credits" — [Filmora](https://filmora.wondershare.com/video-editor-review/zeemo-review.html)
- MixCaptions: yearly fee buys only watermark removal, transcription is metered on top — Paulab1210, Jahcdhvf, Sarjeantnp — [justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/952426779/mixcaptions-video-subtitles/reviews)
- Submagic quotas: Starter $19/mo "capped at 15 videos per month with a 2-minute limit per clip"; Pro $23/mo, 40 videos, 5-minute clips — [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/submagic-pricing-2026); "each plan has a cap on the number of videos per month and their maximum duration, requiring an upgrade or additional purchases if exceeded" — [costbench](https://costbench.com/software/ai-video-editing-saas/submagic/hidden-costs/); one Reddit user quoted second-hand: "I've used submagic in the past but their pricing is crazy (costs as much as Riverside)" — [ucstrategies](https://ucstrategies.com/news/submagic-review-2026-pricing-features-is-it-worth-it-for-creators/)
- Zubtitle: 30-minute cap per video even on the top plan; 212 credits stranded at cancellation — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/183309/Zubtitle/reviews/)
- Aside: Opus Clip and Klap are also minute-metered, but out of scope here.

### Inferences
- The complaint is not "credits are expensive" but "I already paid and there is still a meter, and the meter counts my failures". An ad line like "No credits. No minutes. Nothing to run out of." addresses the exact wording in these reviews.
- Captions' Mirage pivot (credits spanning avatars, dubbing, AI edit) is what put caption-only users on a meter sized for generative video. That is a specific moment of churn for people who only wanted captions.

### Gaps
- No first-hand Reddit/X threads on the Mirage credit change were retrievable; the Submagic Reddit quote is second-hand via a blog.
- Zeemo's exact credit-per-minute rate for plain captioning (as opposed to AI video) was not found in a user review.

## Q5. Demand for batch captioning, offline use, or no-signup

- Free Android caption apps with no watermark are scarce: AutoCap and Kaptioned both watermark the free tier — [Wondershare round-up](https://videoconverter.wondershare.com/subtitle/auto-caption-app.html); Zubtitle free is watermarked and 2 videos/month — [sendshort](https://sendshort.ai/guides/zubtitle-review/)

### Inferences
- Batch is a creator-workflow need that single-clip app reviewers do not know to ask for; it is better shown than claimed in an ad (twenty thumbnails, one tap).
- Offline matters to users as "it works on the train / it never says processing failed" more than as privacy; the ad framing that matches review language is reliability.
- "Unlimited single-clip export with a small watermark" directly answers the Android free-tier pattern (ads + watermark + daily cap). The absence of ads is itself a differentiator on Android, where AutoCap's and Zeemo's top complaint is ads.

### Gaps
- No creator-forum thread asking for batch captioning on mobile was found; the search tool could not reach Reddit.
- No data on how many users of the cloud apps would switch for offline; nothing quantitative exists publicly.

## Q6. Refunds, "charged after trial", weekly-charge complaints

Trial-to-charge and billing-after-cancel are the most severe complaints in the category and they appear for Captions, Submagic, AutoCap, SubCap and MixCaptions. Captions' recent Trustpilot 1-star stream (Aug–Sep 2026) is almost entirely this. Weekly-plan complaints specifically were not found; the anger is about continued charging and silent renewals of any cadence.

## Ranked list: frequency versus severity (for choosing ad lines)

### Takeaway
Ranked by how often the theme appears across apps (frequency) and by how angry and costly it is (severity). The top of both lists is money mechanics, not caption quality, and every one of the top four is something Wordburn structurally cannot do to a user.

### Cited Findings
| Rank (frequency) | Complaint theme | Apps where it appears | Severity | Wordburn answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Credits/minutes consumed, including by failed runs | Captions, Zeemo, MixCaptions, Submagic (quotas), Zubtitle (30-min cap) | High | No credits, no minutes, no caps on paid |
| 2 | Charged after cancel / renewal without notice / trial converts | Captions, Submagic, AutoCap, SubCap, Zeemo, Zubtitle | Highest | Play-managed sub; free tier is full product + watermark |
| 3 | Processing never starts / export blocked / outage / crash mid-run | Captions, Submagic, AutoCap, Kaptioned, Zeemo, Captions Lite | High | No server to be down; no "processing failed" |
| 4 | Ads and watermark in free tier (Android) | Zeemo, AutoCap, Kaptioned, Zubtitle | Medium | No ads; small corner mark only |
| 5 | Support unreachable | Captions, Zeemo, Submagic, Kaptioned | Medium | n/a (one dev; do not over-promise) |
| 6 | Accuracy / word order | AutoCap, Captions (2023), Zeemo | Medium-low | Dictionary; edit never moves timing |
| 7 | Feature gaps: words-per-line, fonts, RTL, cut/paste, no audio while editing | Captions Lite, AutoCap, Zeemo | Low | 18 styles, words-per-line control, audio on every editing surface |
| 8 | Card/account required to try | SubCap | Medium | No account |
| — | Privacy of uploaded footage | none voiced | — | Secondary line, not hook |
| — | Export ≠ preview, timing drift | none voiced | — | Not an ad line; nobody complains in these words |

Sources: all rows derive from the citations in Q1–Q6 above.

### Inferences
- Ad priority from this evidence: (1) "Nothing to run out of" (credits/minutes), (2) "Nothing is processing on a server" (outage/queue/failed), (3) "No ads, no account, no card" (Android free-tier pain), (4) batch and auto clip as *shown* capabilities rather than claimed ones, (5) styles as the visual proof. Preview-equals-export and acoustic emphasis are real strengths with no complaint-shaped demand behind them; they belong in the demo footage, not the headline.
- Praise themes (ease, accuracy, time saved) are what the ad must *demonstrate*; complaint themes are what it should *say*.

### Gaps
- All frequency rankings are qualitative across roughly 60 quoted or summarised reviews; no app exposed per-topic counts.
- Reddit/X/TikTok creator voice is absent from this evidence base for tooling reasons, not because it was checked and found empty.
