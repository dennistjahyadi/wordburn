# Caption-first apps: plan structure, prices and usage limits (October 2026)

Scope: caption-first apps only, global USD. Every number carries its platform
and the date it was read. All vendor pages and store listings below were fetched
on **2026-10-02** unless a different date is given. Google Play refused to serve
its listings to the fetcher, so **no Play "in-app purchases $x–$y per item"
range was verifiable for any app**; where a Play price appears it comes from a
third-party mirror and is flagged. The App Store exposes its full IAP list, so
most store-side numbers here are iOS.

Reference plan under test (Wordburn): $5.99 weekly / $9.99 monthly / $39.99
yearly (= $3.33 per month), 7-day trial on yearly only, no credits, no minute
caps, unlimited exports; free tier exports with a watermark.

Aside, excluded by brief: CapCut, VN and InShot are full editors and Opus Clip,
Klap and Vizard are clipping suites priced per upload minute; none appears in
the tables.

## Key question 1 — Plan prices per app (weekly / monthly / yearly, trial)

### Takeaway
Among mobile caption apps the monthly price clusters at $4.99–$9.99 and the
yearly at $29.99–$79.99; Wordburn's $9.99 monthly is at the top of that band
and its $39.99 yearly ($3.33/mo) is near the bottom. Only two caption apps were
found selling a weekly plan at or above $5.99 (Captions Basic weekly $5.99,
SubCap weekly $7.99); Kaptioned's weekly is $3.99. The web-first tools
(Submagic, Zubtitle, Zeemo web) are priced two to seven times higher per month.

## Key question 2 — What each paid plan includes and caps

### Takeaway
Three models coexist: credits (Captions, Zeemo, Submagic), hard per-month
video counts with a per-video length ceiling (Submagic, Zubtitle, Zeemo's old
plans), and minute packs (MixCaptions). The $5–$10 mobile apps (AutoCap,
SubCap, Kaptioned) publish no caps at all, which reads as unlimited but is
unverified; Captions' credit schedule lists no cost for captioning itself.

### Cited Findings
- Captions: credits are consumed by Prompt to Video (16 per 6 s), AI Edit (10–40), GenAI image/video/music, chat editing (2 per message, 1 per action), sound features; the schedule **does not list captions, subtitles or transcription** as consuming credits; balance capped at 3× the monthly allowance on Basic and above; free credits do not refresh — [Captions Help: Understanding Credits](https://captions.ai/help/docs/ai-usage). Free plan includes "captions… no watermarks" — [Captions Help: Subscriptions](https://captions.ai/help/docs/subscriptions). Max adds Chat to Edit, AI Edit, Clips, Mirage actors, up to 30 AI Twins, 2 concurrent generations — same page.
- Captions App Store reviews report that after about six AI projects a month on the (old) Pro plan "users may experience wait times of up to 360 minutes for additional uploads" — [App Store: Captions](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/captions-ai-edits-your-video/id1541407007). A review claim, not a published cap.
- Zeemo: Pro 3,600 credits/yr, 3-min max, 500 MB, 1080p, 15-day storage, **no watermark removal**; Expert/Business 7,200 credits/yr, 5 hrs / 5 GB, 4K, 30-day storage, watermark removed, priority — [Zeemo pricing](https://zeemo.ai/pricing). Credit rate per minute: "Standard captioning uses 1 credit per minute… translation 8 credits per minute" — [CapCut resource page on Zeemo](https://www.capcut.com/resource/zeemo-ai) (third party); contradicted by "approximately 20–30 credits per minute" — [Bestaitoolspot](https://bestaitoolspot.com/tools/zeemo-ai/) (third party). Zeemo's own page states only "$0.032/min" — [Zeemo vs Rev](https://zeemo.ai/alternative/rev-vs-zeemo).
- Submagic: Starter 15 videos/month, 2-min max, 1080p 30 fps, 45 credits; Pro 40 videos, 5-min max, 1080p/2K, 120 credits; Business 100 videos, 30-min max, 4K 60 fps, 300 credits, unlimited users; "Every price is per member"; API minute packs 250 min $57 up to 10,000 min $1,000 — [Submagic pricing](https://www.submagic.co/pricing). "Each video requires 3 credits at every tier" — [Soku review](https://soku.ai/alternatives/submagic/review); [Cutsnap Submagic pricing](https://cutsnap.ai/blog/submagic-pricing-2026). No rollover policy published.
- Zubtitle: Guru 10 videos/month, Agency 30/month, 20-min max on every tier, up to 4K, uploads under 1.0 GB H.264 — [Zubtitle pricing](https://zubtitle.com/pricing).
- MixCaptions: minute packs as priced above; individual subscribers up to 10 min per video, Business up to 30 min — [App Store: MixCaptions](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mixcaptions-video-captions/id952426779).
- Kaptioned: paid plans remove the watermark; no minute or video cap published; per-video $2.99 unlock — [App Store: Kaptioned](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kaptioned-automatic-subtitles/id1518425078).
- AutoCap, SubCap: no caps, minutes or resolution limits published in either listing — [App Store: AutoCap](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/autocap-auto-video-captions/id1466743701); [App Store: Subcap](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/subcap-captions-ai-subtitles/id1571349902).
- Offline AI Auto Captions Pro: unlimited daily projects, batch of up to 10 videos, larger models, styled ASS export — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.bytesweavers.offlineautocaptions).

### Inferences
- If Zeemo's rate is 1 credit per minute (the figure consistent with its old 300- and 600-minute Expert/Business caps and with 3,600 credits ≈ 300 min/month), Pro gives 300 captioned minutes a month and Expert 600. If 20–30 credits per minute, Pro gives 10–15 minutes a month, which would make an $8 plan useless; the lower rate is far more plausible but is not confirmed by Zeemo.
- "Unlimited" captioning with no minute cap is offered, as far as published pages show, by Captions Basic ($9.99/mo, captions not metered), AutoCap ($4.99/mo or $8.99 once), SubCap ($7.99/mo), Kaptioned ($9.99/mo) and Bytesweavers Pro (one-time). Wordburn's "no credits, no minute caps" is therefore matched on paper by the cheap mobile tier, and only the web tools cap volume hard.
- The cloud apps that publish no caps are the ones most likely to have unpublished throttles (Captions' review-reported 360-minute queue is the example), which is a difference Wordburn can state honestly: on-device, the limit is the phone.

### Gaps
- Zeemo's credit-per-minute rate, rollover and whether Pro really ships a watermark (web page says not removed; App Store description implies removal).
- Actual throttles or fair-use ceilings on AutoCap, SubCap, Kaptioned and Captions Basic: nothing published.
- Contents of MixCaptions "Short video subscription" ($2.99) and "Transcribe videos for Pros" ($9.99): the listing names them without describing them.

## Key question 3 — Free tiers

### Takeaway
Every caption app has a free tier; most watermark it and cap it by count or
length. Captions is the outlier with no watermark on free (it monetises
generative features instead). Wordburn's watermark-only, otherwise uncapped
free tier is more generous on volume than any of them.

### Cited Findings
- Captions Free: 60–200 lifetime credits, captions included, "no watermarks" — [Captions Help: Subscriptions](https://captions.ai/help/docs/subscriptions).
- Zeemo Free: 120 credits/yr, 1-min video, 200 MB, 720p, watermark — [Zeemo pricing](https://zeemo.ai/pricing). Older third-party description: 3 videos/month, 1-min max, 720p, watermark — [Diffyweb 2025](https://www.diffyweb.com/zeemo-ai-review-2025/).
- Submagic Free: 3 videos/month with watermark, 200 MB and 1 min 30 s per video — [Cutsnap Submagic pricing](https://cutsnap.ai/blog/submagic-pricing-2026); [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/ai-video-editing-saas/submagic/free-plan/).
- Zubtitle Bootstrapper: 2 videos/month, 720p, watermark, "free forever" — [Zubtitle pricing](https://zubtitle.com/pricing).
- MixCaptions: first video free up to 3 minutes; standard version carries the MixCaptions watermark — [App Store: MixCaptions](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mixcaptions-video-captions/id952426779).
- Kaptioned: free exports carry a watermark; "2 videos (a total of 20 minutes) for free each day" — [Google Play: Kaptioned](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kaptioned.app&hl=en) (from the listing's description as summarised in search; not re-read directly).
- SubCap: without a subscription "users can manually add subtitles… but auto-generated subtitles require a subscription" — [Capterra: SubCap](https://www.capterra.com/p/236693/Subcap/).
- Offline AI Auto Captions: 3 projects per day free — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.bytesweavers.offlineautocaptions). Whether free exports are watermarked: not stated.
- AutoCap: free-tier limits not stated in the listing — [App Store: AutoCap](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/autocap-auto-video-captions/id1466743701).

### Inferences
- Wordburn free (unlimited, watermarked, full resolution) beats Zeemo (1 min, 720p), Submagic (3/month, 90 s), Zubtitle (2/month, 720p) and MixCaptions (one video) on every axis, and beats Kaptioned on resolution parity only if Kaptioned's free tier downgrades, which is not published. Captions free is the only one that is better on the watermark axis and worse on nothing except that it is a different product.

### Gaps
- AutoCap and SubCap free-tier watermark and resolution: not published.

## Key question 5 — Price rises and moves to credits, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Three of the apps in scope changed model in the window: Captions went
freemium (January 2025), rebranded to Mirage (4 September 2025) and renamed
Pro→Basic and Scale→Frontier with credits as the meter; Zeemo moved from
video- and minute-capped plans to credit allowances and lowered its yearly
per-month figures; Submagic meters videos as 3 credits each. Zubtitle's
prices did not move.

### Cited Findings
- Captions switched to a freemium model in January 2025 and rebranded as Mirage on 4 September 2025 — [TechCrunch, 2025-09-04](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/04/captions-rebrands-as-mirage-expands-beyond-creator-tools-to-ai-video-research/); Mirage raised $75 M on 24 March 2026 — [TechCrunch, 2026-03-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/mirage-raises-75m-to-continue-building-models-for-its-ai-video-editing-app-captions/).
- Help centre lists "Basic (formerly Pro)" and "Frontier (formerly Scale)" — [Captions Help: Subscriptions](https://captions.ai/help/docs/subscriptions). Date of the renames: not published.
- Max price appears as both $24.99 and $29.99 across Captions' own pages in September 2026 — [Cutsnap](https://cutsnap.ai/blog/captions-ai-pricing-2026); [Toolsurf](https://www.toolsurf.com/captions-pricing-2026/).
- Zeemo 2025 plans as described by third parties: Pro $9.99/month, 50 videos/month, 3-min max, 1080p; Expert $14.99 with 300 minutes/month; Business $29.99 with 600 minutes/month — [Diffyweb 2025](https://www.diffyweb.com/zeemo-ai-review-2025/); [Findmyaitool 2025](https://findmyaitool.io/tool/zeemo-ai/). Zeemo's page on 2026-10-02: Pro $8/month billed yearly with 3,600 credits/yr, Expert $13.39, Business $16.07 — [Zeemo pricing](https://zeemo.ai/pricing). Date of the change: not found.
