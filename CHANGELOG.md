# Changelog

What changed for the people using Wordburn. The Play release notes for each
version are cut from here (500 characters; see `PLAY-CONSOLE.md` §8).

## Unreleased — Wordburn Pro

**Batch captions.** Pick up to 30 clips at once, choose one language and one
caption style, and Wordburn works through them in the background, one after
another. A queue screen shows where every clip is, with retry for any that
fail. Finished clips go to a Wordburn album in your gallery, named after the
clip they came from. The queue pauses by itself if your phone runs short of
space or gets too hot, and says why.

**Auto clip.** Pick a long video — a podcast, a stream, a talk — and Wordburn
reads it and suggests up to 10 clips of 20 to 60 seconds that open strong and
end cleanly. Play any suggestion, move its start and end a word at a time or by
dragging, cut out long pauses and stray "um"s with Remove dead air, add your
own, and send the ones you want to the batch queue. Reads the first hour of an
English video, or the first half hour in the other languages.

**Four more languages.** Spanish, German, Dutch and Indonesian, alongside
English. Pick the language the clip is spoken in before you pick the video.
The four share one speech model that is downloaded once (874 MB) the first
time you choose one of them; Settings → Languages can remove it again.

**Saved looks.** Name a caption style — "podcast", "client A" — from Settings
→ Default style, and pick it for a whole batch.

**Wordburn Pro** is now a subscription — weekly, monthly or yearly, with a free
trial on yearly where Google Play offers one. Pro removes the watermark and
unlocks batch captions, auto clip, the four new languages and an unlimited
dictionary. Single clips still caption and export without paying, with a
small watermark in the corner. Anyone who bought the earlier one-time unlock
keeps everything, including all of the above.

Also: very long words — German and Dutch compounds especially — now shrink to
fit the frame instead of running off its edges.
