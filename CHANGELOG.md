# Changelog

What changed for the people using Wordburn. The Play release notes for each
version are cut from here (500 characters; see `PLAY-CONSOLE.md` §8).

## Unreleased — Wordburn Pro

**The app speaks your language.** Everything Wordburn says — every screen,
button and message — is now in Dutch, French, German, Indonesian, Italian,
Polish, Portuguese or Spanish as well as English. The first screen asks which,
starting on your phone's own language, and that choice is also what your
first video is assumed to be spoken in. Settings → App language changes the
words; the Spoken language row on Home still decides each clip.

**Batch captions.** Pick up to 20 clips at once, choose one language and one
caption style, and Wordburn works through them in the background, one after
another. A queue screen shows where every clip is, with retry for any that
fail. Finished clips go to a Wordburn album in your gallery, named after the
clip they came from. The queue pauses by itself if your phone runs short of
space or gets too hot, and says why.

**Eight more languages.** Dutch, French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Polish,
Portuguese and Spanish, alongside English. Pick the language the clip is
spoken in before you pick the video. The eight share one speech model that is
downloaded once (874 MB) the first time you choose one of them; Settings →
Languages can remove it again.

**Saved looks.** Name a caption style — "podcast", "client A" — from Settings
→ Default style, and pick it for a whole batch.

**Wordburn Pro** is now a subscription — weekly, monthly or yearly, with a free
trial on yearly where Google Play offers one. Pro removes the watermark and
unlocks batch captions, the eight new languages and an unlimited
dictionary. Single clips still caption and export without paying, with a
small watermark in the corner.

Also: very long words — German and Dutch compounds especially — now shrink to
fit the frame instead of running off its edges.
