Using Wordburn

Wordburn makes captions for your videos on your own device. You can caption and export single clips without paying; those exports carry a small Wordburn watermark. You are responsible for the videos you caption and for having the right to use them, and for checking captions before you publish them: speech recognition makes mistakes, and Wordburn gives you the tools to fix them.

Wordburn Pro

Wordburn Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, sold and billed by Google Play on a weekly, monthly or yearly plan. It removes the watermark and adds batch captioning, auto clip, captions in Spanish, German, Dutch and Indonesian, and an unlimited dictionary. The price and the length of each plan are shown in the app before you subscribe, in your own currency, exactly as Google Play charges them.

  • Renewal. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the same price, until you cancel.
  • Free trial. Where a free trial is offered, you are charged when it ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Each Google account can have one trial.
  • Cancelling. Cancel anytime in the Google Play Store app, under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from Settings → Wordburn Pro → Manage subscription in the app. Pro stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.
  • Price changes are announced by Google Play before they apply to you, and you can cancel before they do.

If you bought the earlier one-time Wordburn unlock, you keep everything Pro includes, including features added later, without subscribing.

Language models

Captions in Spanish, German, Dutch and Indonesian need a speech model that is downloaded once, on request, from Hugging Face. It is large, so the app asks before downloading it and you may prefer to use Wi-Fi.
